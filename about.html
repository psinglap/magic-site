Why I'm building Magic

I couldn't see my own growth.

I've built three products from zero: an online school that reached students across India, a creator-marketing engine, and now Magic. Each time I learned faster than at any point in my life: data infrastructure, AI agents, enterprise sales, positioning. But none of it was visible. Not to investors, not to the people I hired, and honestly not even to me. I'd read forty tabs on a problem, solve it, and a month later couldn't say what I actually knew.

So I built a recorder for myself. It runs on my laptop, watches how I work, and every night turns it into an honest picture: where I stand in each area, what I've really mastered, how I think, where I'm stuck, and the one thing to do next.

Three weeks in, it knew things about me that no personality test ever had. It noticed that I polish when I should ship, and that I learn best by building something, not by reading. That's when I knew this wasn't a side project.

Everyone deserves that mirror, and it should belong to them, not to an ad platform. That's Magic.
